OTTOTREE AUDIT — WEB USER GUIDE
================================

This guide describes the normal web workflow from account setup through audit
closure and reporting. The pages available to you depend on your role and the
permissions assigned to your account.

1. SIGN IN AND SET UP YOUR ACCOUNT
----------------------------------
1. Open the Audit App website and sign in with the account provided by your
   administrator.
2. Open Account to update your name, email, profile picture, and signature.
   Change your password there if needed. Your signed-in account is recorded as
   the auditor; you do not need to type your name into an inspection.
3. If you cannot sign in, use Forgot Password to send a reset request. An
   administrator must review the request and reset the password.

2. COMPLETE FIRST-TIME ADMIN SETUP
----------------------------------
An administrator should configure these records before staff begin auditing:

1. In Users, create accounts and assign each person's role and department.
   Configure role permissions and inspection capabilities as required.
2. In Outlets, add each outlet, then add its locations, floors, areas, and
   zones. Set display order where needed.
3. In Fixed Assets, add the equipment and assign it to the correct outlet and
   location. Configure the inspection criteria for each asset.
4. In Settings, configure audit types, finding categories, and priority levels.
   Each priority level has a classification and a due-day target.
5. In Settings, review audit scoring, pass mark, rating bands, and category
   weights. Configure the company name, facilities header, and report logo if
   these should appear on reports.

3. SCHEDULE AN AUDIT
--------------------
1. Open Inspections and choose Schedule Work, or use Schedule Visit from Today.
2. Select the outlet and location, set the date and auditor, and add any
   scheduling remarks. Save the schedule.
3. For an audit that is ready to start immediately, use + New Audit. Enter the
   outlet, audit date and time, audit type, and remarks. The signed-in account
   is recorded as the auditor. The app creates a scheduled draft and reserves
   an audit reference.

4. START OR RESUME THE GUIDED INSPECTION
----------------------------------------
1. Open Inspections. Guided Inspection initially shows Scheduled Work.
2. Select the scheduled work item. The app creates or resumes its inspection
   and displays the inspection reference and details.
3. Work through each listed location and fixed-asset criterion.
4. Mark each criterion as passed or N/A when appropriate. For a failed
   criterion, leave it unchecked and enter the required remark.
5. Add a photo for each applicable asset. You can add multiple photos, remove
   a photo, or open Mark to annotate it before saving the mark.
6. When a criterion is failed, the Record Audit Finding dialog opens. Select
   its category, priority, department, and PIC as appropriate; add the cause,
   recommendation, required action, remarks, and evidence. Choose Save Finding
   to Draft to keep these details with the inspection. The finding and linked
   work order are created when the inspection is completed.
7. Choose Save Progress to retain an unfinished inspection. Return to Scheduled
   Work and select it again to resume.
8. When every applicable criterion is resolved and has evidence, the button
   changes to Complete Inspection. Select it to submit. The app validates the
   checklist, calculates the configured score, saves the audit and its findings,
   and updates the dashboard.

5. ADD SIGNATURES AND REVIEW HISTORY
------------------------------------
1. In the inspection, use Audited By, Verified By, or Acknowledged By to add
   the signatures your workflow requires. Select Save Signatures. Your identity
   and timestamp are recorded with the signature.
2. Open History & Findings to browse completed inspections and their findings.
   Use search and filters to narrow the list.
3. Open an inspection and use Export PDF to download its report. The PDF
   includes the audit summary, checklist, findings, available photos, and
   signatures.

6. HANDLE CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
----------------------------
1. Open Work Orders to review assigned findings and other work orders. A
   Department/PIC account sees only records assigned to that PIC or department.
2. Open a work order to review the issue, evidence, due date, priority, and
   assignment. Add comments to its timeline as work progresses.
3. The assigned PIC records the action taken, completion date, completion
   remark, and completion photo before setting the work to Completed.
4. A permitted verifier reviews the evidence. The verifier can set Verified
   with a verification remark, or return the work to In Progress with a remark
   explaining what still needs attention.
5. After verification, close the work order. Closed records are retained for
   audit history.
6. The Notifications page shows relevant progress, assignment, verification,
   due-soon, and overdue notices.

7. CLOSE AN AUDIT AND REVIEW REPORTS
-----------------------------------
1. A verifier can close a completed audit from its history record after all
   required signatures are present and every linked finding/work order is
   closed. A closed audit is read-only.
2. Open Reports to review scores, findings, overdue work, outlet comparisons,
   and charts. Use the date and outlet filters to narrow the report.
3. Use Export PDF on an inspection for its detailed audit report. Use the
   Reports page export actions for CSV, Excel, or JSON report data.

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
- Save Progress frequently when working in the field.
- A failed criterion needs a remark. Applicable inspection items need photo
  evidence before the inspection can be completed.
- Priority due dates come from the priority-level setup. The app sends in-app
  assignment and due-date reminders while the server is running.
- The web application stores its persistent records and image bytes in SQLite.
- The Android app currently keeps its own offline local data. Synchronization
  between Android and the web application is not available yet.
- AI detection, CMMS, WhatsApp, email, and push integrations are future work
  and are not part of this workflow.
