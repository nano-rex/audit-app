AUDIT APP — WEB USER GUIDE
================================

This guide describes the normal web workflow from account setup through audit
closure and reporting. The pages available to you depend on your role and the
permissions assigned to your account.

1. SIGN IN AND SET UP YOUR ACCOUNT
----------------------------------
1. Open the Audit App website and sign in with the account provided by your
   administrator.
2. Open Account to update your name, email, profile picture, and signature.
   Choose a file for the picture or signature, or draw your signature in the
   box and choose Use this drawing; then Save Account. Images appear as
   squares beside their Choose file square; x removes one.
   Change your password there if needed. Your signed-in account is recorded as
   the auditor; you do not need to type your name into an inspection.
3. If you cannot sign in, use Forgot Password to send a reset request. An
   administrator must review the request and reset the password.
4. If your account was created without a password of its own, or an
   administrator reset it, you are asked to choose a new password as soon as
   you sign in. Nothing else is available until you do.

2. COMPLETE FIRST-TIME ADMIN SETUP
----------------------------------
An administrator should configure these records before staff begin auditing:

1. In Users, create accounts and assign each person's role and department.
   Configure role permissions and inspection capabilities as required.
   Each role also has an Outlet access setting. With All outlets its users
   see every outlet. With One outlet (Operation Manager, PIC, Captain) or
   Selected outlets (Regional Manager), choose each person's outlet or
   outlets, either in the role editor (Outlets for each person, listing
   everyone with that role) or in the person's user editor; they then see and
   change only those outlets, in every list, report, and export. These four roles belong to the Operation
   department.
   Each role can report to another role (Reports to, in the role editor);
   a role cannot report to itself or to a role below it. Users >
   Organization draws the chain of command. People (the default view) shows
   each person as a card with their profile picture (or initials), name,
   title, role, and outlets, under the person they report to: someone in the
   role above who covers the same outlet. People whose roles are not in a
   chain are listed below it. Roles shows the same chains by role, with the
   people in each. A profile picture is added under Account. Removing
   a role moves the roles under it up to the role it reported to.
2. In Outlets, add each outlet, then add its locations, floors, areas, and
   zones. Set display order where needed. When editing a location, tick the
   assets and fixtures that belong in it (use the filter to find them);
   unticking one removes it from the location.
3. In Assets, register what will be inspected at each outlet and location:
   - On the Fixed Assets tab, Add Fixed Asset for equipment that has a code
     and serial details (speakers, screens, amplifiers).
   - On the Fixtures & Finishes tab, Add Fixture or Finish for parts of the
     building itself (wall paint, floor tiles, water pipes, toilets, sinks).
     These need only a name, location, category, and condition.
   Give each item a category and review its inspection criteria. A code is
   generated when you leave it blank.
4. Under Assets > Categories, set the department responsible for each category.
   Findings in that category are assigned to it unless the auditor chooses
   another. Audit types and priority levels are managed by the Super account
   in Super Settings.
5. In Settings, review audit scoring, pass mark, rating bands, and category
   weights. Configure the company name, facilities header, and report logo if
   these should appear on reports.

3. SCHEDULE AN AUDIT
--------------------
1. Open Inspections and choose Schedule Work, or use Schedule Visit on the
   Dashboard.
2. Select the outlet and date, and under Assigned to tick the person or
   people who should do it (for example a supervisor assigning staff). Only
   people who can audit and whose outlets include the visit's outlet are
   listed. Each person assigned is notified, and the visit waits for them
   under Waiting on you until it is started. Anyone with audit permission
   can still start it. Add any scheduling remarks. Under Locations, All locations is ticked: the audit covers the
   whole outlet. Tick one or more locations instead to audit only those; the
   inspection then shows only their tabs. Untick them all to return to All.
   Save the schedule. A new schedule is always Pending; it becomes In Progress
   when the audit starts and Completed when it is finished.
   Tick Only mine above Scheduled Work to see just the visits assigned to
   you and the drafts you started.
   To remove a visit, choose Edit and Delete. A visit that was already
   opened has a draft audit, which is deleted with it (you are told first);
   a visit whose audit is completed is kept with that audit record.
3. For an audit that happens now, choose Schedule and start now instead of
   Schedule. The signed-in account is recorded as the auditor, an audit
   reference is reserved, and the checklist opens straight away. Audit time,
   type, and remarks can be set at the top of the checklist.

4. START OR RESUME THE GUIDED INSPECTION
----------------------------------------
1. An audit started with Schedule and start now is already open. Otherwise
   open Inspections; Guided Inspection shows Scheduled Work.
2. Select the scheduled work item, or choose Open beside "Continue inspection"
   under Waiting on you on the Dashboard. The app creates or resumes the
   inspection and displays its reference and details.
   Scheduled Work lists unfinished audits too, including drafts started
   without a schedule. Each audit is named the same way here and in History:
   its audit number and name (for example AUD-2026-0002 · AQP_2026-08-28_11),
   with the schedule it came from (Schedule SCH-00012, or No schedule) in
   the details line. A schedule nobody has started reads "Not started".
3. Work through each listed location and the criteria of each item in it.
4. Use Show and Category above the checklist to work through one kind of item
   or one category at a time, for example only Plumbing. The location and zone
   percentages then count the items shown; the whole checklist must still be
   finished to complete the inspection.
5. Tick each criterion that passes, or choose Pass all on an asset to tick
   every criterion that has no remark. For a failed criterion, leave it
   unticked and enter a remark, or select Finding details beside it.
6. Add a photo for each asset. You can add multiple photos, remove a photo, or
   open Mark to annotate it before saving the mark. Select a photo's thumbnail
   to view it: scroll, pinch, or use + and − to zoom, drag to move around, and
   double-click to zoom in or back out. Previous and Next step through the
   photos shown together, and a marked photo can be compared with its original.
   The same viewer opens from the Photos button on work orders, findings, and
   the asset register.
7. Finding details opens the Record Audit Finding dialog (it also opens when
   you untick a criterion). Select the category, priority, department, and PIC;
   describe the problem; add the cause, recommendation, required action, and
   evidence. Choose Save Finding to Draft to keep these details with the
   inspection; a summary appears under the criterion. The category and
   department start from the item's category and that category's responsible
   department; a failed criterion with only a remark uses those defaults. The
   findings are recorded when the inspection is completed; no work is ordered
   until a work request is raised for them (section 6).
8. Choose Save Progress to retain an unfinished inspection. Return to Scheduled
   Work and select it again to resume.
9. When every criterion is ticked or has a remark, and the required photos
   are attached, the button changes to Complete Inspection. An asset with a
   failed criterion always needs a photo; whether every other asset needs one
   is set by an administrator under Settings > Workflow options. Locations
   with nothing to inspect show "None" and do not count. Select the button to
   submit. The app validates the checklist, calculates the configured score,
   saves the audit and its findings, and updates the dashboard.

5. ADD SIGNATURES AND REVIEW HISTORY
------------------------------------
1. Completing an inspection opens it on Inspections > Sign-off. Sign-off
   lists every completed audit that is not yet closed, with the signatures it
   still needs. Open one to see a card each for Audited by, Verified by, and
   Acknowledged by.
2. On a card you may sign, choose Use my saved signature (the one under
   Account) or Draw signature to draw or upload one. Each signature is saved
   at once with your name and the time. The auditor's card is for whoever ran
   the inspection.
3. Open History or Findings (under Inspections) to browse inspections and
   their findings. Use search and filters to narrow the list.
4. Use Export PDF or Export Excel on Sign-off, in History, or in an open
   inspection to download it. The PDF includes the audit summary, location
   grading, findings, and signatures; the Excel file has the audit, every
   check with its result and remark, and the findings.
5. To report on one location over time, choose its Outlet and Location in the
   History filters (and From/To dates if wanted), then Location PDF or
   Location Excel. It covers every completed audit of that location: an
   overview of the audits, then each audit's checks, photos, findings, and
   signatures there.

6. REQUEST AND ORDER WORK
-------------------------
1. Inspections > Findings lists each item (fixed asset or fixture) that failed
   a check, once, with the checks it failed, their remarks, and photos. It
   shows items that are not closed; change Status to see the rest.
2. Choose Create work request on an item. The request starts with the item's
   department, category, priority, the failed checks as the work needed, and
   its photos; adjust them and choose Submit Request. The item then shows its
   request number (WR-...). Something that was not found in an audit can be
   requested with Add Work Request on Maintenance > Work Requests.
3. Maintenance > Work Requests lists open requests (change Status for the
   rest); they also appear under Waiting on you as "Review work request" for
   whoever assigns work. Choose Create work order to turn a request into a
   work order, filled in from the request: set the PIC and due date and save.
   Or choose Decline with the reason when no work is needed; its findings
   close with that reason.
4. Maintenance > Work Orders lists the work orders. The Dashboard lists those
   assigned to you or your department under Waiting on you ("Resolve work
   order"). A Department/PIC account sees only records assigned to that PIC or
   department, and cannot create or decline orders.
5. Open a work order to review the issue, evidence, due date, priority, and
   assignment. Add comments to its timeline as work progresses.
6. A work order moves through Open, Assigned, In Progress, and Pending, and
   is Closed when the work is done. The person or department it is assigned
   to, or an auditor or administrator, closes it; today's date is recorded,
   and its request and findings close with it. The Status list offers only
   the allowed steps. Deleting an unstarted work order returns its request to
   Open.
7. Closed work orders are kept for audit history and open read-only. If a
   change is refused, the reason is shown at the bottom of the dialog.
8. The Notifications page shows relevant progress, assignment,
   due-soon, and overdue notices. Open goes to the inspection or work order a
   notice is about and marks it read; Mark all read clears the rest. The
   number of unread notices is shown on the Notifications button, or on the
   page menu when that button does not fit.

7. CLOSE AN AUDIT AND REVIEW REPORTS
-----------------------------------
1. A verifier can close a completed audit on Sign-off (or from its History
   record) after all
   required signatures are present and every finding is closed (its work
   order closed or its request declined). It appears under Waiting on you as "Close audit" when it is ready,
   and as "Sign as ..." while your signature is missing. A closed audit is
   read-only.
2. Open Reports to review scores, findings, overdue work, outlet comparisons,
   and charts. Use the date and outlet filters to narrow the report. Reports
   (and the Location PDF and Excel in History) need the Reports permission on
   the person's role; without it the Reports button is not shown.
   Reports also shows how people act:
   - Time to Act: the average and longest time from starting to completing
     an audit, from raising a work request to acting on it (a work order
     created, or declined), from opening a work order to closing it, and from
     completing an audit to closing it.
   - People: per person, the audits they started, completed, signed, and
     closed, the work requests they raised and acted on, the work orders
     they created and closed, and their average times.
   - Activity Log: every step with who took it, when, and how long it took;
     choose a person to see only theirs.
   These follow the report's dates and outlet, and are in the PDF and Excel
   exports. Activity from before this log existed is filled in from the
   records, as far as they show who acted.
3. Use Export PDF or Excel on an inspection for its detailed audit report,
   and Location PDF or Excel in History for one location. Use the Reports
   page export actions for CSV, Excel, or JSON report data. If an export
   says a package is missing, the server is running without the project's
   packages; see "Starting the server" in production-readiness.md.

8. SUPER ACCOUNT
----------------
The Super account is kept in its own database, separate from every
organization. It never appears in an organization's Users list, cannot be
given to an organization user, and its sign-in history and page order are
kept with it. It has no department, profile picture, or saved signature; draw
or upload a signature when signing an inspection. Switching to another
organization keeps the Super account signed in.

The Super account has every page above, plus two restricted pages
under the page menu: Super Dashboard (users, roles, assets, open findings, and
the active company database) and Super Settings (company databases, the
organization theme, priority levels, audit types, scoring, and report
settings). Switching the company database signs every user out.

Each company database is an organization with its own theme. Under Super
Settings > Organization theme, choose a palette (Ottotree follows the teal and
greys of its PM checklist reports), optionally your own accent colour, the font
(Noto Sans SC matches those reports), corner style, density, the default
appearance (light, dark, or follow the device), and whether each user may pick
light or dark for themselves. Changes are previewed as you make them and apply
to everyone, including the sign-in page and PDF reports, once saved. The report
logo under Report & System is also shown beside the organization's name.

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
- Appearance (light, dark, follow device, or the organization's default) and
  items per page are under Settings. The organization may fix the appearance
  for everyone, in which case the choice is shown but cannot be changed.
- Save Progress frequently when working in the field.
- A failed criterion needs a remark. Every inspected asset needs photo
  evidence before the inspection can be completed.
- Priority due dates come from the priority-level setup. The app sends in-app
  assignment and due-date reminders while the server is running.
- The web application stores its persistent records and image bytes in SQLite.
- The Android app currently keeps its own offline local data. Synchronization
  between Android and the web application is not available yet.
- AI detection, CMMS, WhatsApp, email, and push integrations are future work
  and are not part of this workflow.
